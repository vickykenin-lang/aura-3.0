## Operating cadence

- When business execution is authorized, generate exactly ten candidates per daily content batch.
- Quality-gate all ten; never use a self-assigned score.
- Show only dual-pass candidates with score at least 7.
- Founder normally publishes only the best one or two.
- Reject is permanent for that candidate ID.
- Publication is FOUNDER_ONLY during the controlled pilot.
- Publication success requires externally verified Instagram media evidence, not merely approval or workflow success.
- Runtime liveness is governed separately by the adaptive heartbeat policy; heartbeat frequency is not content-publication frequency.
